### [MAJOR] QA17-1 · Settlement — a party's objection reaches nobody, while their signature reaches the operator twice

**Seat:** raised as `performer.b@` and `performer.a@`; read as `operator@`.
**URL:** `/events/e2e00000-0000-4000-8000-0000000000e1/settlement`

**What I did.** As `operator@`, computed, sent for review and finalized the Album Release. As
`performer.b@`, raised a dispute on their own line. Separately, as `performer.a@`, raised a dispute
on Spring Warmup and then signed it off. Then went back into the `operator@` browser seat and
opened the bell, the Dashboard, the Settlements list and the event's Settlement tab.

**What I saw.** The operator's bell holds **one** notification — *"Marlo Vance signed off their
settlement · One more party has agreed their figures."* Neither dispute produced anything:

- no notification, in-app or by email;
- the Dashboard attention list still reads *"You have 4 things that need attention today"*, the
  same four booking/hold rows as before the disputes;
- the event's **Approval Status** roster prints **Neon Tide · Performer · Pending** — the same word
  as the four parties who have simply not answered — and the pill above it reads *"Finalized —
  figures and rates locked"* with no mention that a party has objected;
- the Settlements list row for the Album Release reads **Finalized**, because the operator's own row
  is finalized and only the disputing party's moved.

The only surface that carries it at all is the event's **Event History** feed, eleven rows down:
*"These figures were disputed · Finalized → Dispute · Neon Tide · 18m ago"*.

**Expected.** A dispute is the one thing in the review machine that asks the operator to *do*
something, and the app's own vocabulary says so — `settlementStatusToDisplay` maps `dispute` to the
design system's **`cancelled`** tone, and the party-side caption calls it *"an objection on record"*.
An approval already notifies the operators, by design and with a comment explaining why
(`routes/settlement.ts:4049` — *"'Has everyone signed off yet?' is the operator's question"*).
The objection is the same question answered **no**.

**Actual (the line, verified).** `apps/api/src/routes/settlement.ts:3208` —
`if (status === "pending_review") { … notifyUsers … }`. That is the only `notifyUsers` in the
status route, so `dispute`, `comments_received` and `revised` notify nobody. The comment above it
reasons only about the e-mail to the objecting party — *"a dispute is raised BY a party and mailing
them their own objection helps nobody"*, which is true and is not about the operator.

**Evidence:**

- `docs/screenshots/qa-2026-10-01-run17/operator-bell-has-the-signature-not-the-objection.png` —
  the bell, the green *"Finalized — figures and rates locked"* pill and the rail in one frame.
- ```

    200 {"status":"dispute","updated":1,"emailed":[]}      ← emailed: []
  $ select type, count(*) from notifications … -- no settlement.dispute type exists
  ```
- ```
  $ select coalesce(pr.name,'(representation)'), s.status from settlements s … where event_id='…e1';
     Astra Booking Agency | finalized
     Marlo Vance          | finalized
     Neon Tide            | dispute      ← nobody was told
     Northlight Presents  | finalized
     Priya Sound          | finalized

**Scope.** Reproduced twice, on two events, from two different performer accounts, and read from
the operator seat both times. The co-host seat is equally uninformed (its own row reads
`finalized`). `comments_received` and `revised` are silent for the same reason, but a comment does
at least land in the Comments thread; a dispute has no thread of its own.

**Severity is a judgement call and I want to be explicit about it.** No number is wrong and no
boundary is crossed — this is a missing channel, not bad data. I have graded it MAJOR because the
settlement is the product's contract-of-record, an objection is the only way a party can refuse it,
and the operator can be finished, paid out and gone before they ever scroll the feed.

`finalized`, `POST …/settlement/compute` → 409 *"its figures are locked to the snapshot"*, and
`POST …/settlement/finalize` → 409 *"This settlement is already finalized"*. Only the signature

### [NOTE] QA17-12 · The seed gives the co-host "Operator — full", including `event.delete`

`event_participants…bb` (Northlight Presents, `co_host`) carries the same 24-capability permission
set as the host, including `event.edit`, `event.delete`, `settlement.edit` and

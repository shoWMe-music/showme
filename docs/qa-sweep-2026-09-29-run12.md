## 2. Findings

### [MAJOR] Deals tab — a partly-signed agreement states the old rule and offers an edit the server refuses

**As:** `operator@` (The Lantern Hall), on the Album Release event workspace → **Deals**.

**Steps**
1. `POST /events/<e1>/deals` — a two-party guarantee, "QA12 seal probe".
2. `POST /deals/<id>/send`.
3. `POST /deals/<id>/confirm` as `performer.b@` — **one** of two signatories signs. The deal stays
   `agreement_status = 'sent'`.
4. Open `/events/<e1>?tab=deals` as `operator@` and expand the card.
5. Press **Write terms**, type anything, press **Save terms**.

**Expected.** The figures and the terms sealed at that first signature — this is the run-11 fix, and it
holds at the server (proved below). The card should say so, and should not offer a terms editor.
`apps/api/src/routes/deals.ts` says as much in its own comment: *"`termsAreSealed` is asked by the Deals
tab too, so the screen cannot offer an edit the route will refuse."*

**Actual.** The expanded card reads, verbatim:

> **Terms live until every party signs**

…directly above a live **Write terms** button. The dialog that opens repeats the same superseded rule:

> "Plain text. Every party to this deal sees the same words, and they are **frozen into the signed record
> once everyone has confirmed**. shoWMe does not compute anything from them."

Saving produces a toast (`[aria-live]` region):

> "A party has already signed this agreement, so agreementBodyText cannot change — their signature is on
> the figures as they stand. Reopen it for renegotiation first, which tears every signature up: POST
> /deals/36ff1176-1333-4f85-bdbb-383bad290d67/reopen"

**Evidence**
- Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/qa-2026-09-29-run12/qa12-1-write-terms-refused.png`
- The same refusal from the API, so it is not a client wobble:
  ```
  $ node .claude/skills/verify-e2e/api-as.mjs operator PATCH \
      /deals/36ff1176-1333-4f85-bdbb-383bad290d67 '{"agreementBodyText":"QA12 probe terms text"}'
  409  {"error":{"code":"conflict","message":"A party has already signed this agreement, so
       agreementBodyText cannot change — …"}}
  ```
- `agreementBodyText` is in `SIGNED_TERM_FIELDS` (`apps/api/src/lib/deal-confirmation.ts:150`), so the
  editor can never succeed on a sealed deal.
- Row state at the time: `select agreement_status from deals where id='36ff1176…'` → `sent`;
  `deal_parties` → one `confirmed_at` set, one null.

**The lines that are one predicate behind** (all three ask the *status*, not `termsAreSealed`):
- `apps/web/src/components/DealAgreementCard.tsx:110` —
  `const frozen = agreementStatus === "confirmed" || agreementStatus === "signed";`
  which drives the caption at `:331`.
- `apps/web/src/components/EventAgreementTab.tsx:225-229` — `canEditTerms` is
  `authority.canCompose && deal.agreementStatus !== "confirmed" && deal.agreementStatus !== "signed"`,
  under a comment that states the superseded rule: *"The terms are live until the last signature and
  frozen after it."*
- The same hook one file over already has the right answer — `useEventAgreements.ts:179`
  `const sealed = termsAreSealed(deal, deal.parties);` — and uses it for `canReviseTerms`, `canReopen`
  and `canDelete`. Only the terms editor and the caption were left on `frozen`.

**A third surface, same rule.** The **Event History** tab prints *"Terms frozen at this confirmation"*
only against the **last** signature (`apps/web/src/components/eventHistory.ts:174`, reading an audit
`termsFrozen` that is true only on the status transition). On the reopened `d1` the history therefore
reads *"A party confirmed the agreement · 1 of 3 parties confirmed"* — with no freeze note — at the
moment the terms actually sealed, and *"Terms frozen at this confirmation"* two signatures later.

**Scope.** Reproduces for any deal that is `sent` with at least one signature, for any account holding
`agreement.manage` — the state the run-11 fix exists to create, so it is reachable on every ordinary
multi-party deal between the first and last signature. Not affected by the reseed state: it is a
predicate, not data.

### [MINOR] `hiddenCount` counts cancelled deals, so the co-host is told the night costs more than it does

**As:** `co.host@` (Northlight Presents), Album Release → **Budget Planner** → shared ledger.

**Steps**
1. On the Album Release there are two deals: `d1` (confirmed) and "QA12 seal probe" (**cancelled**).
2. Open the shared ledger as the co-host, who is a party to neither.

**Expected.** A cancelled agreement pays nobody — the engine filters it out
(`ne(status, 'cancelled')`) — so it adds nothing to what the night costs, and the disclosure should
not count it.

**Actual.** The Results panel reads:

> "**2** of this event's deals are not shown to you, so what the night costs is higher than the total
> above. Profit, margin and break-even are left out rather than calculated without them."

One of those two is cancelled and contributes zero.

**Evidence**
```
$ node .claude/skills/verify-e2e/api-as.mjs co.host GET /events/<e1>/deals
{'deals': [], 'hiddenCount': 2, 'hasSettlement': True}

showme=# select name, status from deals where event_id='e2e…e1';
 Album Release — Door Split | confirmed
 QA12 seal probe            | cancelled
```
`apps/api/src/routes/deals.ts:536` — `hiddenCount: deals.length - visible.length`, where `deals` is the
unfiltered `select().from(schema.deals).where(eq(eventId))` at `:503-506`. The status filter the engine applies is
not applied here.

**Scope.** Any reader who cannot see a cancelled deal on an event. Cancelling is the *normal* ending for
an agreement somebody has already seen (decisions §25.7.2), and `useDealAutoSend` makes `cancelled` the
common outcome on the wizard path (§25.7.2's own 2026-09-29 note), so this will drift upward over an
event's life. Suggested one-line fix: filter `deals` by `ne(status,'cancelled')` before both the
visibility split and the count.

## 5. Probes that lied

Five, and each would have become a wrong finding.

1. **"The Deals tab claims a settlement exists when none does."** My first query joined `settlements` to
   `event_participants`, which silently dropped the representation settlement (`participant_id IS NULL`).
   The Album Release *did* carry a computed settlement — the agent's commission, `commission: "300000"` on
   `commissionableIncome: "3000000"`, which also cross-checks against Marlo's 60% of SEK 50,000. The
   sentence was true. Re-query without the join before believing a "no rows" result.
2. **"AUDIENCE LOGISTICS is published despite the screen saying it is not."** Reading the Profiles form
   as flattened `innerText` put the heading *"Kept back for booking conversations / Off your public
   page"* directly under `AUDIENCE LOGISTICS`, when it is the **next** section's heading.
   `VenueDetailsFields.tsx:58` declares `audienceLogisticsNotes: { audience: "public" }`, and
   decisions #19 says in as many words that it *stays public*. The API was right and my reading of the
   DOM was wrong. `innerText` flattens away exactly the grouping a privacy form depends on.
3. **"The co-host's shared ledger hides the cost lines."** The four cost rows looked empty because their
   labels and amounts are `<input>` values, which `innerText` does not return. Reading `input.value`
   showed all four present and editable (`Sound & production` = `12000`, etc.). The co-host sees the
   whole shared ledger, as PLAN.md:215 requires.
4. **"The co-host is offered settlement powers it does not have."** Northlight is offered Recalculate,
   Finalize, Send for review and the whole curation panel on the host's event. It holds them: the seed
   grants it `operator_full` on that event. `PUT /settlement/curation` and `POST /settlement/compute` as
   `co.host` both answered **200**. This is §25.7.4's own trap — *"the sentence 'a co-host can cancel the
   host's show' describes a defect and a granted permission equally well"* — and the only way to tell is
   to read `event_participants.permission_set_id`, which no screen shows.
5. **"Marking a night unavailable does nothing."** A synthetic `element.click()` on the day cell wrote no
   row. A full `pointerdown/mousedown/pointerup/mouseup/click` sequence at the element's own coordinates
   wrote `profile_unavailability | 2026-11-10 | 2026-11-10` immediately, and repeating it removed the
   row. The feature works; my first click did not.

And one non-finding worth recording so the next sweep does not chase it: the Budget Planner's
**SEK 1,245 "loss"** on an otherwise break-even night is the **documented default** payment-processing
assumption — 1.5%, filled in for every budget until the operator says otherwise
(`useBudgetEditor.ts:1216`, *"Every budget assumes a provider takes something until the operator
says otherwise"*). `planning_assumptions.paymentProcessing` was `null` in the DB, which reads like a
figure appearing from nowhere until you find the comment.

1Public profiles — two directions, plus preview mode

Today the public profile is the private profile with the edit buttons hidden: a cover, a bio, and a list. It doesn't do the one job it has — turn a stranger who found the link into a booking request. Both directions below lead with a single action (Request a date), put live availability from the shoWMe calendar above the fold, and carry the trust signals only shoWMe can issue (verified party, rider on file, shows settled). 1a and 1b are the same performer, same content, opposite treatments. 1c stretches the winner-agnostic system to a venue, 1d is the in-app preview mode, 1e is the phone — where most shared links are actually opened.

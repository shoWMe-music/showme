3. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. They are widely used to:

  • Enable functionality
  • Improve performance
  • Analyze usage
  • Personalize content

We may also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and tracking scripts.

4. Types of Cookies We Use

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the platform to function. They enable:

  • Account login and authentication
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Core platform functionality

These cookies cannot be disabled.

4.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies improve usability and user experience. They may store:

  • Preferences and settings
  • Interface configurations
  • Session information

4.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the platform. They are used to:

  • Measure usage and performance
  • Identify issues and improve functionality
  • Analyze workflows and user behavior

Data collected may be aggregated and pseudonymized. shoWMe uses Google Analytics for this purpose; these cookies are only set after you provide consent.

4.4 Product Intelligence and Data-Driven Features

shoWMe uses cookies and similar technologies to support its data-driven platform. This includes:

  • Understanding user behavior across features
  • Improving matching, analytics, and recommendations
  • Supporting AI and algorithm development

Where required by law, these cookies are only used with your consent.

4.5 Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies or process data, each under their own privacy and cookie policies:

  • Google Analytics for analytics (consent-based). Policy
  • Google Cloud & Firebase for hosting, authentication, and file storage (strictly necessary). Policy
  • Stripe / Mollie for payment processing and fraud prevention. Stripe · Mollie

Some of these providers (including Google) are based in the United States, so accepting analytics cookies may involve a transfer of data outside the EU/EEA, safeguarded by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses. See our Privacy Policy for details.

5. Legal Basis for Cookie Use

We use cookies based on:

  • Strict necessity for essential platform functionality
  • Legitimate interest for basic performance and security
  • Consent for analytics, product intelligence, and non-essential tracking

Where consent is required, cookies will only be activated after you provide it.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences by:

  • Opening your cookie settings on this site
  • Adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies

You may withdraw consent at any time.

7. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

If you disable certain cookies:

  • Some features may not function properly
  • Analytics and personalized features may be limited
  • Data-driven services and recommendations may be reduced

8. Data Protection

Cookie data is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Use cookies to directly identify you beyond necessary functionality

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date. Your consent to non-essential cookies is based on your choice in the cookie banner (not on your continued use of the site) and you can change or withdraw it at any time.

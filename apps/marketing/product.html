The product

One event.
Every side of it.

From the first booking request to the final settlement, shoWMe runs the whole event in one shared, multi-role system. Every side works on the same event, and sees exactly their own slice of it: the dates they're on, the deal they signed, the settlement that pays them.

01 · Incoming requests

Every booking request in one inbox.

Offers and booking requests land in a single, structured inbox, no more digging through email and DMs. See the act, the date, the fee and the room at a glance, then make an offer, save a draft, decline, archive or flag it. Duplicate requests for the same date collapse into one, and offers nobody answers expire on their own.

  • Structured requests with date, fee, venue & capacity
  • Offer, draft, decline, archive or flag as spam
  • Both sides notified the moment you respond
Incoming Requests
02 · Availability & holds

Publish when you're open. Hold what you're deciding.

Mark yourself unavailable, then share your open dates with the collaborators you choose, down to which days of the week count and whether holds block a date. Holds are ranked, so a first and second hold on the same night is explicit: the operator sets the rank, the performer confirms, and when a hold drops the next one is promoted automatically.

  • Range, calendar & days of the week
  • Choose whether held dates read as unavailable
  • Ranked holds, promoted automatically when a date frees up
Check & Share Availability
03 · Calendar

One shared calendar for the whole event.

A single calendar that every side of the event reads from: venues, performers, promoters and crew. Switch between month, week and day, filter by performer or venue, and watch suggested, pending, held and confirmed dates update across everyone's calendars. Door, set and curfew times are stored as local wall-clock time against the venue's timezone, so nothing shifts when a date moves.

  • Month, week & day views
  • Colour-coded status across every party
  • Local door & set times, anchored to the venue timezone
Calendar · month view
04 · Budget planner

Know the numbers, and who's holding them.

Model the whole event before it happens: ticket tiers, itemised costs, revenue, profit and break-even updating live. Every revenue line records who collects the cash and every cost records who fronts it and who it's for, which is exactly what makes settlement automatic later. Co-promoters share one budget; keep a private one beside it, or save the whole thing as a template.

  • Live revenue, costs, profit & break-even
  • “Collected by” and “Paid by” on every line
  • Shared budget for co-promoters, private one for you
Event · Budget Planner
05 · Deals & agreements

The deal is the agreement.

The terms you agreed become the contract (fee, split, escalators, date, room) filled straight from the deal, with nothing retyped. Each party confirms their own side; the moment the last one does, the terms freeze as the record. Reopening takes an approval and leaves a trace, and a PDF is always one click away.

  • Guarantee, door split, guarantee-vs-door or rental
  • Each party confirms their side; terms freeze on confirmation
  • PDF any time, e-signature on the roadmap
Event · Agreement
06 · Team & tasks

Your team, your crew, and everything to do.

Bring your own people into shoWMe (booking, production, sound, door, box office) each with access to exactly what they need. Roles are per profile, so someone can be an admin on one and a viewer on another. Reusable groups drop a whole crew onto an event in one action, and only admins take a seat: viewers and crew are free.

  • Per-profile roles: owner, admin, editor, viewer, crew
  • Reusable groups: add the whole crew in one action
  • Tasks by group or person, with due dates and reminders
Team & groups
07 · Collaborators

Every side of the event, and only their side.

This is the heart of shoWMe. Instead of a system built for one role (venue only, agent only, promoter only) the venue, the performer, the agent and the promoter all work on one event. Visibility follows the relationship: the operator running the event sees the whole picture, co-promoters sharing a budget see it with them, and every arm's-length party sees their own deal and their own settlement. A performer never sees another performer's split, and a rental venue never sees the acts' fees.

  • Venues, performers, agents, promoters & crew on one event
  • Party-scoped by default: each side sees only their own deal
  • Off-platform parties held in place until they claim their profile
Event · Collaborators
08 · Settlement

Everyone settles from the same numbers.

shoWMe works out who owes whom: what each party earned under their deal, minus the cash they already collected or fronted on someone else's behalf. The nets always cancel out, so the math can't quietly drift. Comment, revise and approve in one thread; on finalise the figures are frozen as a record and invoices come off the same lines. shoWMe tracks the money; it never holds it.

  • One settlement per party: each sees their own
  • “Who owes whom”, recomputed live from the budget
  • Mark paid or already handled; invoices from the same lines

09 · Audience

Keep the crowd you earned.

Every published event collects RSVPs (name, email, city) into a list that belongs to your profile, not to a ticketing company. See who keeps turning up across your events, where they're based, and export the whole thing whenever you want.

  • RSVPs captured on your public event pages
  • One list per profile, deduplicated by email
  • Yours to export, no lock-in
Audience
10 · Reports

Every event makes you smarter.

Because the whole event already lives in one place, reporting is a by-product rather than a chore: revenue, costs, profit and margin per event, and a projection across your confirmed pipeline. Setlists your performers file turn into the PRO report you have to submit anyway.

  • Revenue, costs, profit & margin per event
  • Projections across your confirmed pipeline
  • Setlists → PRO reports for STIM, GEMA & PRS
